# DBpedia text: derived evidence in Git

This directory retains the completed 13-method aggregate, frozen protocol, 13 global configuration choices, 130 calibration-choice records, the whole-study confirmation gate, seven saved-record CSV projections, and original plus portable independent verification receipts. It contains no text content, tokens, feature vectors, raw prediction arrays, pretrained weights, trained checkpoints, or private operational logs.

`raw_evidence_catalog.json` is the exact catalog of the separate complete local weight-free archive (1,193 payload files). Raw predictions are retained in that local archive and are not uploaded here. `derived_evidence_manifest.json` lists the files actually present in this Git directory. Full numeric replay requires the local archive and its unchanged original paths; this small derived set alone does not provide raw replay inputs.

For Git presentation only, `git_record_mapping.json` binds all 143 renamed records to their original bundle paths, byte counts and SHA-256 digests. Thirteen global `hyperparameters.json` records are copied into `frozen_choices/<method>__hyperparameters.json`; 130 per-seed `calibration_choice.json` records are copied into `calibration/<method>__seed_<seed>__calibration_choice.json`. No file bytes or original raw archive paths change. The mapping is not a replacement for the raw archive catalog or its replay layout.

All 13 methods use ten final optimization seeds (10-19), after two seed-9 tuning trials per method. The projections retain 190 confirmation readouts, 100 official-test exploratory readouts, 4,060 per-class F1/recall rows, 480 calibration candidate scores, 114 summary metric rows, eight primary paired comparisons and 80 paired seed differences. No support columns are invented. Every projection copies completed saved record fields; preparation performs no inference or scientific metric/statistical recalculation. Official-test readouts are exploratory and are unavailable for the six matched CLS methods.

The original independent audit checks all 156 actual checkpoint states (26 tuning plus 130 final), their completion budgets and numeric replay. The portable audit loads zero checkpoint states, explicitly omits all 156, and replays the complete tuning, confirmation, native-test, calibration and eight-contrast records independently from saved numeric captures. Both audited maximum numeric discrepancies are zero. These scopes do not claim encoder re-extraction, model inference replication, or external replication.

The seven frozen-vector/mean-vector arms complete 50 adapter epochs and 5,200 updates; the six matched CLS last0/last1/last2 CE/Balanced-Softmax arms complete three epochs and 318 updates. Text EQ uses coefficient 0.05 throughout all 50 adapter epochs. Each method selects its own learning rate, so objective/EQ contrasts compare selected pipelines and may include learning-rate differences. The selected log-prior decision offsets optimize macro-F1 and do not establish probability calibration. Ten paired seeds quantify optimization variation on one fixed DBpedia partition, without external-data or hyperparameter-selection uncertainty.

`posttraining_execution.json` is a separately scanned operational companion. It preserves the initial MPS checkpoint-RNG decode failure before any MPS confirmation cell was predicted, subsequent completion of the prescribed phases, 360 unchanged CPU confirmation files and unchanged model/optimizer/training/RNG-storage bytes. Its hash-bound external RNG decode adapter is provenance rather than a generic portable model evaluator; the independent numeric replay does not use that adapter. In the complete local archive, operational companions live separately under `companions/text/` to keep the immutable raw `text/` catalog exact.

See `docs/text_followup.md` for the scientific protocol and local archive replay commands. Read the raw catalog before replay and write new receipts outside immutable evidence directories, or work on a disposable copy.
